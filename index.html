MIO

NBRP’s First Original IP

Character

MIO is NBRP’s first original character, personally conceived and designed by the founder of NBRP.MIO is a chubby silver-gray cat with distinctive blue almond-shaped eyes and a signature tuft of silver fur on its forehead. Its soft, rounded form and distinctive facial features give MIO an immediately recognizable visual identity, while its cool-toned appearance creates a striking contrast with its playful and unconventional personality.The character reflects the founder’s own personality, dreams and ambitions, expressed through an exceptionally strong duality — intensely cool and distant on one side, yet playful, witty and full of character on the other.

Personality & Traits

  • Ambitious — Driven by dreams and ambition.
  • Proud — Carries a strong sense of pride and dignity.
  • Arrogant — Bold, self-assured and unapologetically proud.
  • Unconventional — Never plays by conventional rules.
  • Defiant — Refuses to be easily defined or controlled by others.
  • Independent-Minded — Follows its own judgment and standards.
  • Disdainful of Conformity — Holds little regard for blind conformity and ordinary conventions.
  • Playful & Witty — Humorous, playful and unexpectedly mischievous.
  • Extreme Duality — Its contrasting sides are both intensely expressed, leaving almost no middle ground.

MIO’s Motto

“Don’t tell me the rules. I decide how the game is played.”

Design Philosophy

Metal & Water

MIO’s design is guided by the founder’s affinity for the Metal and Water elements. This philosophy influences MIO’s color direction, visual language, atmosphere and overall identity.
